## English (en-US)

**Name:** EngineBuddy

**Short description:**

> Your little AI buddy on the desktop: free, private, no account. EngineBuddy lives on your screen
> as an animated character, answers questions, looks at screenshots, checks your PC, opens
> programs and helps you code. The AI runs entirely on your PC (via Ollama), so your conversations
> never leave it.

**Description:**

> EngineBuddy is a friendly AI companion for your Windows desktop, made with developers in mind.
>
> What it does:
> • Chat with history: reopen earlier conversations and carry on
> • Understands pictures: screenshot button, Ctrl+V or drag and drop (with a vision model)
> • Checks your PC: CPU, memory, free space
> • Opens programs and folders, opens projects in VS Code
> • Reads files in your project folders and changes them after you OK a diff
> • Runs commands, but only after you've seen the exact command and clicked Allow
> • Looks things up on Wikipedia and reads public web pages, naming the source
> • Remembers things about you (see and delete them any time)
> • Reads replies aloud, in English, German, French or Spanish
>
> Free and private:
> • No account, no subscription, no ads, no telemetry
> • The AI runs locally on your PC; your data stays with you
> • Open source (MIT) on GitHub
>
> Safe:
> • Risky actions always need your click; passwords and keys are off-limits
> • Every action is logged
>
> Requires: Ollama (free, ollama.com) and a one-time model download of a few GB. The setup
> assistant walks you through both on first start. Recommended: 16 GB RAM.

**What's new in this version:**

> First-run setup assistant, free local AI via Ollama, Wikipedia search, four languages.

**Product features:**

1. Free AI that runs entirely on your PC
2. No account, no ads, no telemetry
3. Chat with history and pictures
4. Screenshots by click, Ctrl+V or drag
5. PC check: CPU, RAM, disk
6. Open programs, folders and VS Code
7. Read files and change them after your OK
8. Commands only after your explicit click
9. Wikipedia search and web pages with sources
10. Read-aloud in four languages

**Search terms** (max. 7): AI assistant, desktop companion, Ollama, local AI, coding helper, offline chatbot, desktop pet
